## 3. Marketing Claims & MoCRA Responsible Person Verification Gate

- **Vegan, Paraben-Free & Cruelty-Free Claims:** All three marketing badges/claims are suppressed across the storefront UI until signed manufacturer attestations / Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) are archived.
- **MoCRA Responsible Person Statutory Requirement (FD&C Act § 609(a), 21 U.S.C. § 364e):** The cosmetic product label must bear a **domestic address, domestic phone number, OR electronic contact information** (such as a website or monitored email address) through which the Responsible Person can receive adverse-event reports. Both a U.S. street address and domestic phone number are not mandatory in every case. **No placeholder or invented contact details are published.**
- **Owner Confirmations Required Prior to General Availability:**
  1. Who legally qualifies as the **Responsible Person** (manufacturer, packer, or distributor whose name appears on the label under 21 U.S.C. § 364(4)) for each applicable product.
  2. Whose legal entity name appears on the physical product label.
  3. Which compliant contact method will be used (domestic address, domestic phone number, **or** electronic contact information).
  4. Confirmation that the designated contact channel is actively monitored and operationally ready to receive and report serious adverse events under 21 U.S.C. § 364a.
