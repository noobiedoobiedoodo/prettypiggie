# PrettyPiggie.com — 110-Product Canadian Regulatory Review & CNF Workflow (`P0-CAN-01`)

## 1. Initial Launch Market Strategy & Technical Enforcement
- **Active Market Control:** PrettyPiggie.com is configured for a **U.S.-Only Initial Launch (50 U.S. States & D.C.)**. Canadian and international shipping are disabled in the storefront checkout drawer and restricted via `buyerIdentity: { countryCode: 'US' }` in the Shopify Storefront API payload until Canadian regulatory gates are cleared.
- **Health Canada CNF Statutory Timeline (*Cosmetic Regulations* s. 30):** Manufacturers and importers are required to submit a Cosmetic Notification Form (CNF) to Health Canada **within 10 days after first sale in Canada**. Pre-launch absence of a CNF number is not a statutory violation; however, exact ingredient concentration bands from the manufacturer are required to file each CNF within the 10-day window once Canadian shipping is enabled.
- **Bilingual English / French Cautionary Warnings (*Cosmetic Regulations* s. 18 & 24):** Bilingual English and French cautionary advisories (`WARNING / MISE EN GARDE`) are implemented on all 110 product presentation sheets.
- **PIPEDA & CASL Compliance:** Full PIPEDA Fair Information Principles disclosures and an unchecked-by-default CASL Express Marketing Consent control are integrated into the Legal Policy Center and Shopping Bag Checkout Drawer.
